# Creative Writing Prompts

Once upon a time, in a kingdom where dragons and humans lived in peace,

The last lighthouse keeper watched the storm approach, knowing that tonight would change everything.

She opened the ancient book and found a letter addressed to her, dated two hundred years in the future.

In the year 2147, humanity discovered that the universe was not what they thought it was.

The robot looked at its hands and, for the first time, felt something it could only describe as sadness.

Deep beneath the ocean, where sunlight never reaches, a city of glass still glows.

The detective stared at the impossible crime scene: a locked room, no windows, and a victim who had been dead for a century.

When the music stopped, everyone in the ballroom froze except for her.

The garden had been abandoned for fifty years, yet every morning, fresh flowers appeared on the grave.

He was the last person on Earth who remembered what stars looked like before the fog came.
